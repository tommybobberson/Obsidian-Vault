## Text Elements
Notice how the density of points near the mean
are greater, and the density of points further
out decreases. This is a sign of normally distributed 
errors as majority of the error terms are bunched together
(doesn't have to be near the mean itself but that's
just a good frame of reference) ^cV63Iqzd

For a given value of a predictor variable, the points (and thus errors)
are normally distributed around the predicted mean values, with more 
closer to the mean and less further out ^4lYgGQK3
