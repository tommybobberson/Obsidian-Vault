## Thoughts 
- it was really nice having them with us the past week. Spending so much time together and having the chance to bring them about really was a once in a lifetime experience and i doubt we'll get the chance to do this again. Really we've all grown up so fast and it's a shame that we didn't appreciate the time we had together as kids - - when we could meet every year and every holiday but we kinda didn't want to go back, just because we felt it was boring. in hindsight, we don't have that much time with anyone - - baba, mama, laozher, yingsu, suliyi, nainai, gugu, gufu and the list goes on. Though i think i should explore why I feel so much more strongly about the lack of time i have with some people as opposed to others. 
- learning to appreciate is just so, difficult? 

- recently I've been thinking back upon [[new world's end]] and it's quite ironic how much of their story we've mirrored: meeting in the most unexpected of places (or rather times), both full of passion; me wanting to work it out, perhaps only in name, but being unwilling to compromise which inevitably lead to that slow drift. there's something harrowing about how we walked those streets so full of love and hope and admiration for the future, promising that we'd try forever, while we're here now. 
- I mean, new worlds end was great, and i thoroughly adored it but our story doesn't have to end the same way - - i don't want it to end the same way. With one of us being unable to let go, as the other slowly drifts off and does their own thing before, possibly, disappearing forever. 
- I don't want to look back and realise that it was all just a dream.
