***Definition***: The ratio of the strength of your key and your fill light 
- note that your key light/fill light can be flashes or natural light
## Common Ratios
**1:1**: even lighting, no shadows
- works for beauty and fashion shoots

**2:1**: there's a 1 stop difference between the light and shadows
- flash is twice as powerful as the ambient
- for ==newborn or family==; makes even light with subtle shadows

**3:1**: brings out excellent highlights and shadow details
- best for ==black and white portraits==

**4:1**: shadows become deep blacks and the side with the fill light is accentuated
- good for portraits
- has a slimming effect as you're effectively illuminating one side of their body
