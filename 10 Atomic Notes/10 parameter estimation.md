## Overview
Parameter estimation refers to different techniques to determine the most appropriate [[10 parameters]] for (usually) a regression.
- examples include: [[10 OLS|ordinary least squares]], 

This must be done as, it is impossible to know the true value of a parameter, and how it correlates with the specific response variable of interest. Thus we must make educated guesses as to what values [[10 parameters]] take. 

***As they are ==Estimates==, estimated parameters are represented by $\hat{\beta_i}$ instead of $\beta_i$
