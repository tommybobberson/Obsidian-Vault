Right now, whenever I'm posed with a problem, I always think about how it's not my fault, how I should retort to clear my name and importantly and most dangerously **how i imagine i'd fight and clap back**.

As much discipline as it might take, I should stop running these imaginary arguments through my head because:
1. It just makes me angrier and boosts my ego
2. It reinforces the idea of [[i'm always right]]
3. It veers really close to power-tripping and i don't want to do anything that could hurt others, or isn't reasonable to them either. However, if i keep thinking in these ways like: i'll hit them if they hit me -- which is extremely unhealthy. Then, i'm just letting my anger pent up instead of thinking of thinking about how I can solve the problem together with others
## Experiences
- meimei saying she has a problem with me and that we can talk after she get's back -- i'm already speculating about the problem and how it's just her misperceiving it. instead, i should think about it as ==how we can both solve this problem together, and it's a learning experience for me==. Because I truly cannot think of what I did wrong
