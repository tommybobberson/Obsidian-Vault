## When do you need to cite?
- Facts are in the public domain and quoting them is not plagiarism. I.e. the earth is round doesn't need to be cited but ==results from certain studies that other author's have done must be cited==
- anything that is original work

## What constitues plagiarism
- paraphrasing an author's work while retaining much of their paragraph structure. i.e. (He ate lunch to He consumed Lunch)
- ==copying author's original ideas, most often opinions, without citation==
