## Perceived Brightness
- **The perceived brightness of light, diffusely reflected from a surface (i.e. emitted by a surface?) does not scale linearly with the actual reflected light**; 
- Psychophysical experiments indicate that perceived brightness increases approximately as the logarithm of the luminous flux
- A doubling of the actual flux (reflectance), results in an increase of 1.5 units on the **arbitrary** brightness scale
- ![[10 Perceived Brightness-perceived brightness.jpg]]
### Impacts
- you don't perceive the increase in exposure/nits of a screen/photograph linearly?
- 500 nits vs 400 nits isn't that big of a deal
