**When poised with a problem, focus on how you and the other party can move forward to solve the problem -- such as trying to understand each others' feelings' and taking the corrective action -- instead of asserting that you aren't wrong or that you're right to feel a certain way/it's not your obligation to do something some way**

==Think of it as a joint learning experience!==

### Steps
- Right now, you need to stop [[10 imagining how to clear your name]]
- but importantly, acknowledge how the other individual must have felt and ==lay out actionable steps== to solve the problem:
	- e.g. if you're fighting with meimei cause she feels like you aren't walking the dog enough because I always stay late at work, raise ways which would **help her feel like i'm taking on the load in a clear way**, depending on the problem at hand
	- if the problem is that you aren't helping around enough at home you can do other chores like the dishes, wash clothes etc. and/or show how you've already been doing this.
	- if the problem is simply one of balancing dog walks, we can both come to a common schedule on which days we allocate ourselves to walk the dog. This way, we both make the same sacrifices
