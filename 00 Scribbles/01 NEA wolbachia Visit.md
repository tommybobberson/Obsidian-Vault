# Project Owlbachia


## Strategies for disease control using wolbachia 

**Suoressjon**
- Singapore employs suppression, to reduce the level of infectious population to a level that inhibits disease transmission 
- only Male mosquitoes are released - > females bite but males don't bite - for ones comfort 

**Replacement**
- establish a dominant wolbachia aefes population 
- by releasing both **males and females**

**why?**:
- Singapore already has a low level of mosquitoes and as such it's more efficient to employ suppression. Instead of doing replacement and introducing more mosquitoes which will bite and cause discomfort (even if they're not infectious )

## Wolbachia breeding 
- **ensuring wolbachia quality in bred mosquitoes are up to standard**: keep some females to perform pcrs on to make sure wolbachia bacteria is there 
- **mosquitoe monitoring**: in the environment, stagnant water traps -(gravitraps) trap female mosquitoes who go in to lay eggs
	- trying to automate identification and of mosquitoes in the trap
	- designed for urban and thus aedes egypti
- **monitoring in more forested / rural environments**: night catcher
	- uses dry ice and light to bait mosquitoes
	- helps get hourly information on mosquito behaviour
	- ![[01 NEA wolbachia Visit-night trap nea|3000]]- helps monitor anophelis mosquitoes



- trapped mosquitoe samples will be sent to the lah for mosquito iding
- primary dengue vector : aedes aegpyti; also spread zika; More urban mosquitoes
- secondary dengue vector: aedes albopictus, carry wolbachia hy default so they aren't so great for dengue; chik outbreak; garden mosquitoes

**differentiating the gender of mosquitoes**
- males: slightly larger, bush6 antennae, proboscis cannot pierce skin 
- bushy antennae, to help sense the wingbeat freq of the female mosquitoes 
- males and females are seperated are the pupae stage, based on size. females are larger and males ar larger. So they make sure they have sufficient nutrients at the larvae stage to get to their optimum size 
- ![[01 NEA wolbachia Visit-wolbachia pupae size female and male.jpg]]
- larvae eat fish food, it smells really bad 
- sorting is 99.9% accurate, so all male pupae are xrayed. Sterilises females but will not affect the fertility of the males 
- pupae are then packaged into release containers before being released
- - females are pcr tested to see how much wolbachia they still hold and recycled for breeding the next batch of wolbachia 
- number of mosquitoes released at the rate of 6/resident/week

**locations of release**:
- ground floor and across floors in public housing too 
- release points are spread over a "block", 
- pilot trial of releasing wolbachia in dengue outbreak areas 


**genotype stuff***
- females carrying wolbachia mating with males without wolbachia will allow eggs to hatch 
- males carrying wolbachia mating with females without wolbachia do not allow eggs to hatch
